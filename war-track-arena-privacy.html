# Privacy Policy – War Track: Arena **Last updated: September 22, 2026** War Track: Arena is developed and operated by Engin Kutlu / Tengri Games. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the game. ## 1. Information We Process War Track: Arena uses account and online game services. Depending on the features you use, the game may process: * **Account information:** email address, Firebase user ID, and username. * **Game and profile data:** level, experience, in-game gold, avatar selection, gender selection, owned and equipped tank parts, customization choices, and game settings. * **Authentication and multiplayer identifiers:** identifiers required by Firebase Authentication, Unity Authentication, matchmaking, and relay services. * **Technical information:** information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the game and its online services. Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are not intentionally stored by the game in the player profile database. ## 2. How We Use Information Information is used to: * Create and authenticate player accounts. * Save and restore player progress. * Provide multiplayer and matchmaking functionality. * Maintain game settings. * Prevent abuse. * Troubleshoot technical problems. * Operate the game's online services. ## 3. Third-Party Services The game uses third-party services, including: * Google Firebase Authentication * Google Firebase Firestore * Unity Authentication * Unity Multiplayer / Matchmaking / Relay services These providers may process information as necessary to provide the services integrated into the game. Their processing is also subject to their respective privacy policies. ## 4. Data Sharing We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared with the third-party service providers listed above only as necessary to operate authentication, data storage, multiplayer, matchmaking, relay, security, and technical services. ## 5. Data Retention and Deletion Account and game data are retained for as long as needed to provide the game and its online features, unless deletion is requested or a shorter retention period is required by law. For account or data deletion requests, contact us using the email address below. We will review the request and delete applicable account data from systems we control, subject to legal or technical retention requirements. ## 6. Security Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 7. Children's Privacy War Track: Arena is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the operation of the game. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game's data practices or applicable requirements change.

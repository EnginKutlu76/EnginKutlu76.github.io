SlideX - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SlideX handles information when you use the game. SlideX is developed and published by Tengri Games.

2. Information We Collect

SlideX does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as a name, email address, password, or phone number.

SlideX does not use analytics services and does not send gameplay statistics, high scores, or other gameplay data to our servers.

3. High Scores

SlideX stores the player's high score locally on the device. This information is used only to provide the game's high score functionality and is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Advertising

SlideX displays advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. If Google AdMob is used to provide advertisements, Google and its advertising partners may process certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information related to ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, in accordance with their applicable policies.

SlideX does not directly collect or store this advertising information on its own servers.

For more information about how Google uses information when using its partners' apps and services, please review Google's privacy information.

5. Internet Access

SlideX does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay features. An internet connection may be used by the advertising service to request and display advertisements.

6. Data Sharing

SlideX does not sell or directly share users' personal information. Third-party advertising services may process information as necessary to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, third-party services used by the application are subject to their own privacy and security practices.

8. Children's Privacy

SlideX is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

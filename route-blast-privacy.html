Route Blast - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tengri Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Route Blast mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Route Blast uses account and cloud services to provide features such as user authentication and saving game progress.

Depending on how you use the game, the following information may be processed:

  • Account and authentication information used to create and access your game account.
  • Game-related information such as level progress, lives, coins/gold, boosters, and other gameplay data.
  • Technical and usage information collected through Firebase Analytics.
  • Information required to display and measure third-party advertisements.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate your game account.
  • Save and restore your game progress.
  • Provide and maintain the features of Route Blast.
  • Understand how the game is used and improve gameplay and stability.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, or technical problems where necessary.

3. Firebase Services

Route Blast uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Analytics.

These services may process information necessary for authentication, cloud data storage, analytics, application performance, and related functionality.

Firebase data is transmitted using appropriate security mechanisms, including encrypted connections where supported by the service.

4. Advertising

Route Blast displays third-party advertisements. Advertising providers may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information related to ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and advertising personalization, depending on the advertising provider and the user's device and privacy settings.

Third-party advertising providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Analytics

Route Blast uses Firebase Analytics to understand general usage of the application and improve the game.

Analytics may collect information such as app usage events, session information, device-related information, and identifiers used by the analytics service.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Account and gameplay information may be stored using cloud services such as Firebase Cloud Firestore for as long as necessary to provide the game's functionality.

Analytics and advertising information may be retained by the respective third-party service providers according to their applicable policies and retention practices.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers that are necessary for the operation of Route Blast, including Firebase services and advertising providers.

9. Children's Privacy

Route Blast is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate legal basis or parental involvement where required.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by Route Blast. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
